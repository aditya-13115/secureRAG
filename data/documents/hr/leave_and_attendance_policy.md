## Planned Leave

Planned leave should normally be submitted at least three working days in advance. Managers should consider delivery commitments and team coverage when approving requests.

Employees should record leave in the company HR system so payroll and attendance records remain synchronized.

## Unplanned Absence

For same-day absence, notify the manager or team channel as early as possible. Supporting documentation may be requested for extended absence where required by policy.

## Attendance

Hybrid employees should maintain accurate work-location information on team calendars when a location materially affects availability for meetings, customer work, or security requirements.

Repeated unexplained attendance issues are handled through normal manager and People Operations processes.
