## Operating Principles

Make ownership explicit. Every recurring business process should have a named accountable owner and a documented escalation route.

Prefer small, reversible experiments before major commitments when uncertainty is high.

## Decision Quality

Separate facts, assumptions, and decisions in leadership documents. Record what evidence changed the decision so future teams can understand the context.

## Meeting Discipline

Recurring meetings should have a decision or coordination purpose. Pre-reads should be shared early enough that attendees can review data before the meeting.

## Cross-Functional Work

When a project spans departments, define interfaces rather than relying on informal coordination. Shared metrics should be agreed before execution begins.
